Education & Qualifications

I completed a Computer Science BSc degree at City, University of London, graduating with a First-Class Honours in July 2026. My degree has provided me with a foundational knowledge and experience using multiple different languages and tools to produce a product.

A major part of my studies was the development of my dissertation, I produced a data-driven application with a focus on analysing cost of living statistics and trends across regions of England. I used a stack of PostgreSQL to house and model the data and Python to create the application, perform the analysis and to create the visualisations. I also used the OpenAI API to generate personalised insights for some of the visualisations. The project gave me valuable experience and was one of the deciding factors for my want to move into this field.

Before my degree I took part in a triple IT BTEC in college and recieved a D*DD (distinctions).
